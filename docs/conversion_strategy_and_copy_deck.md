# EESA Academy — High-Converting Lead Architecture & Copy Deck
**Strategy:** Complete transition from low-intent "Free Demo Classes" to high-perceived-value "Free 1-on-1 Personalized Language & Career Counselling Sessions."  
**Target Institute:** EESA Academy (*Expert Educational Services Academy Pvt Ltd*, West Delhi)

---

## 1. Strategic Positioning & Perceived Value Refactor

### 1.1 The Psychological & Conversion Value Shift

| Feature / Dynamic | Legacy "Free Demo Class" | New "1-on-1 Strategy & Diagnostic Session" |
|---|---|---|
| **Perceived Value** | Low (Commoditized free sample) | **High (Customized diagnostic audit & prescription)** |
| **User Mindset** | Passive, judgmental ("Entertain me") | **Active, goal-driven ("Help me fix my score/career")** |
| **Attendance Rate** | ~35% – 45% (High ghosting/no-shows) | **75% – 85% (High commitment via pre-diagnostic questionnaire)** |
| **Conversion Close** | ~12% – 18% | **38% – 52% with consultative gap analysis** |
| **Pricing Leverage** | Price resistance; discounts demanded | **Price inelastic; course fee justified by cost of inaction** |

---

### 1.2 Core Deliverable Takeaways

1. **EESA 4-Pillar CEFR/IELTS Diagnostic Audit:** Real-time evaluation of Grammar Syntax, Lexical Resource, Speech Rhythm/Accent, and Task Fluency.
2. **Tailored 60-Day Exam/Fluency Blueprint:** Week-by-week roadmap calculating exact study hours, mock exam milestones, and score targets.
3. **Overseas Visa & University Eligibility Check:** University band requirement mapping for UK, Canada, Australia, USA, and NHS nursing registration.

## 3. Counselor Conversion Protocol & CRM Handoff

### 3.1 Pre-Session Cadence
* **T-24 Hours:** WhatsApp + Email with Calendar invite & Campus directions (Dashrath Puri Metro Gate 1).
* **T-2 Hours:** SMS alert: *"Hi [Name], Senior Counselor has prepared your diagnostic scoring sheet. See you at [Time]."*
* **T-10 Minutes:** Live WhatsApp ping: *"Counselor is ready / Campus desk reserved."*

### 3.2 15–20 Minute Structured Consultation Protocol
1. **00:00 - 03:00 (Rapport & Framing):** Establish consultative authority; set agenda.
2. **03:00 - 08:00 (Live Diagnostic Assessment):** 3-minute speaking prompt & grammar deconstruction.
3. **08:00 - 12:00 (Gap Analysis & Cost of Inaction):** Reveal score deficit and financial/career risk of delay.
4. **12:00 - 16:00 (The Prescribed Roadmap):** Present the customized 60-day syllabus.
5. **16:00 - 20:00 (Enrollment Close):** Present upcoming small micro-batch (max 8 seats) and close seat reservation.
